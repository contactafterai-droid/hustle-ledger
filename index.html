News

What changed, in plain words, with a link to who reported it.

  • Nov 5, 2026

    Rockstar posts a new launch gallery of GTA 6 screenshots

    Two weeks out, Rockstar added a fresh batch of official screenshots to the GTA 6 site, showing Vice City nightlife, wildlife, chases and diving around Leonida. No story details, just a last look before November 19.

    Reported by PC Gamer
  • Nov 4, 2026

    Data firm estimates GTA 6 pre-orders near 7.7 million

    Sensor Tower estimates about 7.71 million console pre-orders worth roughly $751 million, around three quarters on PS5. This is a third-party estimate; Rockstar and Take-Two have not shared sales figures.

    Reported by Kotaku
  • Nov 3, 2026

    Pre-load moves up to November 10 and needs up to 212GB

    Rockstar set digital pre-load for November 10: midnight local time on PS5, midnight GMT on Xbox. Boxed copies hold a code that arrives November 12. Clear up to 212GB. We had listed November 12 for everyone; the facts table is now corrected.

    Reported by Rockstar Newswire
  • Oct 15, 2026

    Rockstar releases the GTA 6 launch trailer

    A 90-second trailer captured on PS5 opens on Jason's exit from the army and Lucia calling him from prison, then runs through heists and chases ahead of the November 19 launch.

    Reported by GameBrief
  • Sep 10, 2026

    Rockstar publishes formal mod rules that will cover GTA 6

    Story expansions and cross-game mashups are banned, and mods can only be sold through storefronts Rockstar approves. No crypto, gambling or selling in-game cash.

    Reported by Game Developer
  • Sep 8, 2026

    NoPixel V opens a closed beta on the Rockstar Games Launcher

    The best-known GTA V roleplay server now launches from Rockstar's own client for about 450 invited players, the clearest sign yet that Rockstar backs roleplay.

    Reported by Runtime Wire
  • Aug 31, 2026

    Early preview adds detail on cars, pawn shops and an in-game social feed

    A streamer Rockstar invited to a hands-off preview described GPS trackers on high-end cars that a mechanic must remove, pawn shops that buy stolen goods, and a short-video social network inside the game.

    Reported by GamingBible
